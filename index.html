Bridging Languages. Empowering Businesses.

IBS (Innovative Business Solutions) offers professional language translation services, document localization, business process outsourcing (BPO), and back-office support in Hyderabad, Telangana, India. Established in February 2021 (5+ years in business) by founder Abburi Tanuja, who brings 15+ years of professional experience.

Language Translation Services & Business Outsourcing Solutions

Based in Kondapur, Hyderabad, IBS supports corporate enterprises across Telangana and India with document translation, business and corporate translation, website and language localization, audio & video transcription, multilingual communication, survey translation, BPO services, back-office administration, data processing, and operational assistance.
